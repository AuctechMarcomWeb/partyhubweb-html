Privacy Policy

How PartyHub Connect collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Introduction

PartyHub Connect ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services. By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

Information you provide during registration and account management.

b. Vendor Information

  • Contact person details
  • Business email and phone number
  • Business location (via Google location services)
  • Portfolio images and service details
  • Bank account details (for payouts and settlements)

c. Booking & Transaction Data

  • Booking details (date, time, location, services)
  • Payment and transaction details (via secure third-party payment gateways)
  • Invoice and billing data

d. Authentication Data

When signing in using Google, we receive basic profile information used to authenticate your account.

e. Communication Data

Messages and call data exchanged between users and vendors using in-app chat and calling features.

f. Device & Technical Data

Technical information required to operate the app reliably on your device.

3. App Permissions

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — used to provide location-based vendor discovery and services.
  • Camera & Media (READ/WRITE STORAGE) — used to capture photos for profiles and vendor portfolios, and to upload and manage images within the app.
  • Audio & Communication — required for in-app voice calling between users and vendors, to optimize audio during calls, and to support audio routing (e.g., Bluetooth headsets).
  • Phone & Call Management (MANAGE_OWN_CALLS & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL) — used to manage call behavior and ensure a seamless in-app calling experience.
  • Device & App Behavior (WAKE_LOCK, TURN_SCREEN_ON, DISABLE_KEYGUARD) — used to ensure incoming calls and important notifications are visible, and to restore notifications and services after device restart.
  • Network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE) — used to maintain uninterrupted real-time communication (calls, notifications).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE) — used to send alerts for bookings, messages, and updates.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide booking and vendor services
  • Enable communication (chat and calls)
  • Process payments and subscriptions
  • Send notifications and updates
  • Improve user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

5. Real-Time Features

PartyHub Connect provides real-time voice/video calling (via third-party services like Zego and socket-based communication). These features require access to microphone, network, and device state permissions.

6. Payments & Subscriptions

  • Payments are securely processed through third-party gateways
  • Vendor subscription plans are available
  • We do not store sensitive card details

7. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Communication service providers
  • Vendors and users (only for booking purposes)
  • Analytics and hosting providers

We do not sell personal data.

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Users can request deletion anytime.

9. Data Security

We apply encryption and industry security practices to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

10. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and improving user experience.

11. Bookings

Bookings become final once accepted by vendors. No cancellation or refund after acceptance.

12. Children’s Privacy

The platform is not intended for users under 13.

13. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted in the app.
