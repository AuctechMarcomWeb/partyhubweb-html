How It Works

Earn, Track, Redeem

Three simple steps — all inside the app.

01

Earn

Earn Rewards points on every completed booking paid through PartyHub Connect — birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and more.

02

Track

Watch your points and tier progress in the PartyHub Connect app. Your tier is based on the points you collect.

03

Redeem

Redeem points toward eligible bookings and offers on your next occasion, right inside the app.

Important: All bookings facilitated through PartyHub Connect must be paid through the platform. Paying outside the platform voids your Booking Guarantee and Rewards points.
