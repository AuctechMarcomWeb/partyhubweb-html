Getting Paid

How Vendor Payments Work

Clear, predictable payouts — so you always know when and how you get paid.

How does the payment process work?

Customers pay securely through the PartyHub Connect app when they book. Funds are held safely until the booking is delivered, protecting both you and the customer — no chasing invoices or cash on the day.

When is the booking fee deducted?

Your plan's completed-booking fee is deducted automatically from each completed, confirmed paid booking before payout — 12% on Starter, 8% on Pro, and 5% on Business. What you see in your vendor dashboard is exactly what you receive — no separate fee invoices. No booking = no fee.

When do I receive my payout?

Payouts are released after the event is completed and are deposited directly to your bank account, typically within 24–48 hours after the event. You can track every payout, deduction, and pending balance in the vendor app.
