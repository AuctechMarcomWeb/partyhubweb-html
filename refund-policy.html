Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work on PartyHub Connect.

1. Booking Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed only after the vendor accepts the request and payment is successfully completed.

2. Cancellation By User

Users may cancel a booking request only before the vendor accepts it. Once a vendor accepts the booking, it is considered final and cannot be cancelled.

3. Refunds

  • Full refunds are applicable only if the booking is cancelled before vendor acceptance.
  • No refunds will be provided after the vendor accepts the booking.
  • Any exceptions (if applicable) will be at the sole discretion of PartyHub Connect.

4. Failed Or Cancelled By Vendor

If a booking is not accepted by the vendor or is cancelled by the vendor, the user will receive a full refund.

5. Refund Processing

Eligible refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days and credited to the original payment method.

6. No-Show

No refunds will be provided if the user fails to attend or utilize the booked service after confirmation.

7. Advisory

Users are advised to review vendor details, pricing, and service terms carefully before confirming a booking.
